About Amal Hostel

More Than Just A Place To Sleep

Established with a vision to provide a safe haven for students of Kottayam Medical College, Amal Ladies Hostel has evolved into a premier residential community. We understand the rigorous demands of medical education, which is why our facility is engineered for peace, privacy, and convenience.

Located in Arpookara, our strategic position ensures you save valuable commute time, investing it where it matters most—your studies and well-being.

  • Strictly Managed & Secure Environment
  • Culturally Diverse & Inclusive Community
  • Zero-Ragging Policy
